# Strategic recommendation - ZOOP Megastore, Q1 2023

**Question:** based on everything analyzed, what should be the main strategic initiative for the next semester?

## Recommendation (English)

We recommend prioritizing Peripherals: 38% of revenue and the top category in 7 of 10 states. Next semester, focus marketing and inventory on this category, starting with SC, RS and GO (48% of revenue) and ensuring availability of the 5 products that drive 46% of revenue, especially the Mesa Digitalizadora (15%). In March, revenue grew only 4% and Peripherals dropped 22% from February, which adds urgency. We do not recommend a Prime conversion campaign for now: subscribers spend 28% less per customer on average (R$ 14.3k vs R$ 19.9k). The sample is small, so review the program's costs and benefits before expanding it.

## Recomendação (Português)

Recomendamos priorizar os Periféricos: são 38% do faturamento e lideram em 7 dos 10 estados. No próximo semestre, concentrar marketing e estoque nessa categoria, começando por SC, RS e GO (48% do faturamento) e garantindo disponibilidade dos 5 produtos que geram 46% da receita, em especial a Mesa Digitalizadora (15%). Em março, o faturamento cresceu apenas 4% e os Periféricos recuaram 22% sobre fevereiro, o que reforça a urgência. Não recomendamos, por ora, uma campanha para converter clientes em Prime: os assinantes gastam em média 28% menos por cliente (R$ 14,3 mil contra R$ 19,9 mil). A amostra é pequena, então vale revisar custos e benefícios do programa antes de expandi-lo.

## Evidence

| Finding | Number |

| Month-over-month growth | Feb +20.3%, Mar +4.1% |

| Peripherals is top category in | DF, GO, PE, PR, RS, SC, SP (7 of 10 states) |
| SC + RS + GO share of revenue | 48.3% |
| Top 5 products share of revenue | 46.3% |
| Mesa Digitalizadora share of revenue | 14.9% (55% above the 2nd product) |
| Avg revenue per buying customer | Prime R$ 14,255.06 (15 customers) vs non-subscribers R$ 19,900.10 (30 customers) |
| Avg ticket per sale | Prime R$ 5,627 vs non-subscribers R$ 7,281 |
| Prime customers who bought | 15 of 15 (100%), vs 30 of 35 non-subscribers (86%) |

## Secondary opportunities

- **Raise basket size where there are many buyers and low spend:** MG, DF and BA have 4 to 6 buyers each spending about R$ 11.5k to 11.9k, versus R$ 23k to 30k in SP, PE, RS and RJ.
- **Acquire more customers where spend per customer is high but the base is small:** SP (3 buyers) and PE (2 buyers).

## Limitations

- No cost, margin, stock or satisfaction data: "most profitable" means "highest revenue", and the inventory recommendation is about prioritizing availability.
- Small sample: the Prime vs non-Prime difference is borderline (Welch t-test p about 0.05, Mann-Whitney p about 0.07). States with 1 to 3 buyers (PR, PE, SP) swing widely with a single large customer.
- Revenue follows the brief (`sale_value * quantity`). If `sale_value` is already the sale total, the category ranking is unchanged but the 3rd product in the Top 5 changes.
